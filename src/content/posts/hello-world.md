Every developer's first program prints "Hello, World." It felt fitting to start this blog the same way.

## Why a blog?

I've been writing code for years, and most of what I've learned came from someone else's post, README, or late-night Stack Overflow answer. This is my attempt to give some of that back — a place to write up things I figure out, decisions I find interesting, and ideas I want to think through in public.

Posts here will mostly be about software: things I'm building, patterns I find useful, and the occasional rabbit hole I fell into and couldn't stop exploring.

## What to expect

No fixed schedule, no content calendar. I'll write when I have something worth saying. Topics will likely include:

- Web development and the tools around it
- Developer experience and tooling
- Lessons from building side projects

If any of that sounds interesting, welcome — glad you're here.
